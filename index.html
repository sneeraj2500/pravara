Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
Our MOQ starts from 100 units per SKU, making it accessible for small retailers and Amazon sellers. For mixed containers (LCL), we can accommodate smaller quantities per product.
Are your toys EN71 and ASTM certified? +
Yes! All our products are EN71 certified for EU markets and ASTM F963 compliant for the USA. We provide full test reports and documentation with every shipment.
Do you offer custom branding / OEM? +
Absolutely. We offer private label and OEM manufacturing. Custom packaging, logo printing, and product color changes are available for orders above the minimum threshold.
What shipping options are available? +
We offer both LCL (Less than Container Load) for smaller orders and FCL (Full Container Load) for larger shipments. Air freight is also available for urgent orders.
What is the typical lead time for production? +
Standard production lead time is 30–45 days depending on order quantity. Samples can be dispatched within 7–10 business days. Rush orders may be accommodated subject to capacity.
Can I get samples before placing a bulk order? +
Yes, we encourage sample orders before bulk purchases. Sample cost is nominal and will be adjusted against your first bulk order. DHL/FedEx shipping available for samples worldwide.
